# Stop conditions

The one manual invocation must stop honestly on provider unavailability,
unsupported contract, identity conflict, budget exhaustion, duration
exhaustion, governed-universe exhaustion, insufficient eligible supply,
handoff failure or any other fail-closed terminal.

It must not lower the $3,000 liquidity floor or any identity, holder, safety,
evidence, cooldown, STNP, rotation or deduplication gate. It must not bypass the
Source Governor or the Central Scheduler. It must not activate retrieval,
paper decisions, BUY/SELL/HOLD, paper positions, trade events, paper trade
audits, PnL, live wallets, private keys, real funds, live execution, paid APIs,
embeddings, vectors, scoring, ranking, confidence or weighted decisions.

`WINDOW_5M_MICRO_EVENT` remains support-only and cannot independently unlock
memory outcomes, retrieval, decisions, positions or PnL.
