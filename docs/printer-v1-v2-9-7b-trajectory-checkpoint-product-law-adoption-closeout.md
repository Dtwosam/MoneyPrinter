## Adopted Product-Law Rules

Printer must treat the full trajectory as first-class memory. A main memory is
not reduced to opening price versus closing price. The spec now requires
ordered intra-window phase preservation where evidence supports it, using only
approved categorical vocabulary.

Outcome direction is separate from evidence quality. Round trips, failed holds,
pump-and-dumps, collapses, negative closes, and dead-token outcomes may be
CLEAN_MEMORY when their evidence is complete, timely, exact-target,
provenance-clean, correctly ordered, and realistically interpretable.
DIRTY_MEMORY remains reserved for untrustworthy evidence.

Each future main memory must preserve the four layers where evidence permits:
full-window outcome, ordered intra-window phases, decision checkpoints, and
realistic action paths. These are future semantics only and do not unlock
decisions.

## Trajectory Evidence and Checkpoints

The clean master spec now requires memory design to preserve or derive supported
trajectory facts: open, high, low, close, time to high/low, favorable and
adverse excursion, drawdowns, recovery, range width, reversal order, time above
and below opening, snapshot color counts, higher-high/lower-low behavior,
phase-level liquidity, volume, transactions, flow, safety changes, and entry or
exit realism.

Unsupported metrics must remain UNKNOWN or UNPROVEN. The factory guide now
places this in anti-bias guidance so future implementation cannot hide gaps,
invent labels, or treat a close-only result as the whole lesson.

## Continuous Decision-Checkpoint and Re-Entry Law

Future paper-decision lanes must evaluate approved scheduled snapshots and
material market events by rebuilding the current setup and comparing it to clean
historical checkpoint memories. Historical checkpoints may use only information
known at that checkpoint.

The docs now prohibit fixed nominal-price imitation. Printer must compare
conditions such as path, reversals, liquidity, entry/exit realism, volume,
transactions, flow, safety, volatility, market context, Solana context, prior
expansion/collapse timing, position state, and similar historical checkpoint
outcomes.

Re-entry semantics are documented as future-only. A token may eventually produce
multiple separate paper trades inside one longer lifecycle, but each re-entry
requires a closed previous position, fresh setup, new clean-memory comparison,
new realism checks, new invalidation and exit conditions, no future
information, and a separately auditable result.

## Anti-Look-Ahead and Exit Realism

The anti-look-ahead rule is strengthened: later recovery, peak, collapse, final
close, or outcome labels may evaluate an earlier checkpoint, but may not support
that earlier checkpoint's simulated action.

Observed peaks are separated from realistically capturable exits. A chart peak
is only a chart fact unless event-time evidence proves usable liquidity, a valid
paper route or quote, acceptable slippage and price impact, sufficient
opportunity duration, no hidden snapshot gap, no wick-only assumption, and a
valid exit rule.

## Lifecycle-Wide 5m Support

WINDOW_5M_MICRO_EVENT is now documented as lifecycle-wide support evidence, not
opening-only evidence. It may be conditionally captured during an ongoing 15m,
1h, or 4h lifecycle after V2-9.7C approves exact categories.

Every 5m support event must exact-link to campaign, run, token, pair, root 15m
lifecycle, containing main window, triggering snapshots, source provenance, and
scheduler work.

The permanent restrictions remain explicit: 5m is conditional, exact-linked,
Source-Governed, Central-Scheduler-led, support-only, audit-visible, and
non-authoritative for main outcomes. It must not replace 15m, become a main
outcome memory, independently trigger continuation, count toward main clean
thresholds, determine cooldown/archive/reopen by itself, unlock retrieval or
actions, create positions or trade events, create paper trade audits, or create
PnL.

## Money-Usefulness Contribution

These rules allow Printer to learn realistic entry, exit, hold, avoid, and
re-entry lessons from the full path of a token instead of treating a profitable
intra-window journey as a simple losing close. Printer can later learn why a
move was tradable, why a hold failed, why a re-entry might or might not have
been justified, and why a chart peak was not necessarily capturable profit.

## Functionality Risks / Setbacks / Efficiency Blockers

| Risk | Why it matters | Required control |
|---|---|---|
| Trajectory over-segmentation | Creates false precision and noisy memory | Approved categorical vocabulary and proof fixtures |
| False reversal labeling | Turns ordinary noise into fake lessons | Evidence thresholds and ordered snapshot checks |
| Snapshot gaps | Hide missing path evidence | Gap-visible reporting and fail-closed quality |
| Wick-only observed peaks | Produces fake perfect exits | Separate observed peaks from capturable exits |
| Fake perfect exits | Inflates paper-profit lessons | Require event-time route, liquidity, slippage, impact, duration, and exit-rule evidence |
| Nominal-price matching | Makes cross-token price imitation look meaningful | Compare conditions, not price X |

| Future-data leakage | Later facts can falsely justify earlier action | Strict checkpoint evidence boundary |
| Excessive checkpoint frequency | Burns source budget and creates churn | Scheduler/event checkpoint limits |
| Repeated re-entry churn | Creates noisy repeated trades | Require closed prior position and fresh comparison |
| Unconditional 5m capture | Turns support evidence into source drain | Conditional trigger categories and negative no-capture proof |
| 5m accidental main outcome | Violates product law | Main-window filters and exclusion tests |
