# V2-9.7D.5B Manipulation-Aware Context Objects Closeout

## Scope

V2-9.7D.5B adds pure, immutable, fail-closed representations for manipulation
lifecycle stages, adjacent stage transitions, the eight approved behavior
families, and frozen checkpoint-time manipulation context. The implementation
reuses the existing memory-quality, safety, holder-distribution, liquidity,
flow, wallet-participation, governed-provenance, trajectory, checkpoint, gap,
identity, lifecycle, main-window, and 4B support-capture contracts.

It performs no source request, scheduler action, runtime orchestration,
persistence, retrieval, decision, execution, or financial action.

## Money-Usefulness Contribution

Printer can now preserve manipulated, mixed, or unknown market conditions
without treating manipulation as an automatic rejection or authorization. The
four independent dimensions retain whether evidence was trustworthy, what
market-integrity condition was observed, whether tradeability was known, and
whether any action was eligible. This prevents useful manipulated-condition
memory from being discarded while also preventing chart behavior or apparent
tradeability from becoming an action license.

## What 5B Improves

- Fixes the ten manipulation lifecycle stages and eight approved behavior
  families as categorical vocabularies.
- Validates only adjacent ordered stage transitions supported by exact
  checkpoint observations.
- Freezes exact campaign, run, cycle, slot, token, mint, pair, root 15m
  lifecycle, containing main window, scheduler work, snapshot, observed-time,
  provenance, trajectory, checkpoint, cutoff, and visible-gap linkage.
- Reuses existing safety, holder-distribution, liquidity, flow, and wallet
  participation labels instead of creating parallel evidence systems.
- Preserves clean, partial, dirty, and do-not-train quality independently from
  market integrity, tradeability, and action eligibility.
- Keeps mixed integrity context mixed and preserves unsupported wallet control,
  coordination, insider status, authenticity, intent, and identity as
  `UNKNOWN`, `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_RESEARCH`, or `CURRENT_EVIDENCE_GAP`.
- Allows exact 4B support evidence to contribute while retaining permanent 5m
  non-authority.
- Rejects stale, mismatched, foreign, post-cutoff, unsupported, untraceable, or
  gap-crossing dependent claims.
- Evaluates later evidence separately without mutating the frozen context.

## What Remains Unknown or Locked

Wallet common control, participant coordination, insider status, participant
authenticity, intent, and identity remain unknown without a later governed
source contract. Manipulation intent, route realism, executable entry or exit,
capturable profit, and future price remain unclaimed.

Opportunity segments, tradeable-path outcomes, entry/exit evaluation, execution
realism, profitability, wallet clustering, identity inference, source calls,
scheduler/runtime orchestration, database migration or persistence, retrieval,
paper decisions, BUY/SELL/HOLD, WAIT, AVOID, NO_ACTION activation, positions,
trades, audits, PnL, wallets, keys, signing, and live execution remain locked.

## Functionality Risks / Setbacks / Efficiency Blockers

- Lifecycle transitions are intentionally limited to adjacent approved stages;
  sparse evidence may block a transition rather than infer an unobserved skip.
- Current wallet and participant evidence cannot establish identity, common
  control, coordination, authenticity, insider status, or intent.
- Categorical safety, holder, liquidity, and flow labels preserve context but do
  not establish execution realism or profitability.
- Dirty or do-not-train evidence remains representable for honest audit context
  but remains dirty or do-not-train; usefulness never promotes its quality.
- Visible gaps block dependent behavior and transition claims, so incomplete
  campaigns may yield less manipulation detail.
- Objects are in-memory contracts only. Persistence and lifecycle integration
  remain outside this lane.
