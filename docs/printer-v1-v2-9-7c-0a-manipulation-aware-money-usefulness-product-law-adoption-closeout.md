## Required Behaviour and Context Coverage

The law document requires all eight manipulation behaviours as mandatory V2-9.7C
design coverage: fast coordinated pump; wash-like or artificial flow;
concentrated early ownership or suspected insider distribution; liquidity add,
pull, lock, or unlock behavior; dev/creator/pool/holder pressure where governed
evidence supports it; trap/wick/late-buy/exit-failure behavior;
dead-to-active/manipulated-to-active/revival behavior; and participant-
authenticity uncertainty.

It also requires all twelve tradeable-path contexts: expansion/pullback/
continuation; expansion then failed continuation; fast breakdown then genuine
reclaim; wick-only peak; price rising while exit deteriorates; high volume with
weak authenticity; good entry with bad hold; bad entry with profitable outcome;
correct exit then more upside; missed entry with no-chase; re-entry churn; and
market-context mismatch.

Four-way separation is preserved: evidence quality, market-integrity condition,
tradeability, and action eligibility are separate dimensions. Two outcome layers
are preserved: full-window outcome and internal trade-opportunity outcome remain
separate.

## Money-Usefulness Contribution

This adoption makes Printer better at learning from the thing memecoin markets
actually do: manipulated-looking moves, noisy paths, traps, wicks, late chases,
round trips, missed entries, and exits that look profitable on a chart but were
not executable. It protects future corpus growth from winner-only memory and
fake profit by making tradeability, action timing, and evidence gaps first-class
policy before design begins.
