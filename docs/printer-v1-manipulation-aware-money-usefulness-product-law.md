# Printer V1 Manipulation-Aware Money-Usefulness Product Law

Status: Binding product-law adoption for V2-9.7C.0A.

This document is the dedicated binding home for the manipulation-aware and
money-usefulness laws assembled by the V2-9.7C.0 readiness audit. It does not
authorize implementation, source fetching, runtime, operational memory growth,
retrieval, paper decisions, BUY/SELL/HOLD, positions, trades, audits, PnL,
wallets, live execution, paid APIs, scoring, ranking, confidence percentages,
weighted logic, embeddings, or vectors.

Printer V1 remains Solana-only, Solana-memecoin-only, and paper-only.

## Manipulation Laws

1. Printer must not automatically exclude a token merely because its activity
   appears manipulated.

2. Manipulation, coordination, artificial activity, concentrated ownership, and
   uncertain participant authenticity are market-context and integrity evidence,
   not automatic action outcomes.

3. A manipulated token may still produce clean memory when its underlying
   observations satisfy clean-evidence requirements.

4. Future approved paper actions may consider manipulated conditions only when
   evidence quality, route availability, liquidity durability, execution realism,
   and exit capability are sufficient.

5. Printer's objective is to distinguish manipulated moves that remain
   realistically tradeable from manipulated moves that trap participants or
   remove realistic exit capability.

6. Printer must be fully disciplined about rules, evidence boundaries, timing,
   and exit requirements. It must not claim certainty about future price.

## Money-Usefulness and Tradeable-Path Laws

7. Full-window outcome and internal trade-opportunity outcome are separate and
   must never be merged. A negative 15m, 1h, or 4h window may contain several
   profitable, realistically tradeable opportunity segments.

8. Chart profit is not executable profit. An observed peak or ATH remains a
   chart fact until event-time route, liquidity, slippage, price impact,
   duration, and exit evidence prove realistic capturability.

9. Money-usefulness diagnostics remain separate fields. They must never be
   combined into a score, rank, confidence percentage, weighted decision system,
   embedding, or vector decision process.

10. Anti-hindsight is mandatory. Checkpoints may use only information available
    at that time. Action paths must be finite and predeclared. Later outcomes may
    evaluate but never rewrite an earlier checkpoint. Exact-bottom, exact-top,
    completed-chart re-entry, and post-window action-path reconstruction are
    prohibited.

11. Operator capital-management preferences may later be optional during bounded
    paper research, but disabling them may never disable a permanent Printer
    safety invariant. Capital-policy changes must be validated, versioned,
    auditable, non-retroactive, linked to governed decisions, and applied only at
    approved safe boundaries. An active losing position may not rewrite the rules
    under which it opened.

## Binding Interpretations

- Printer is not an organic-coin detector. It is a disciplined memory and
  paper-research system that must learn which conditions were realistically
  tradeable, which trapped participants, and which preserved capital.
- Evidence quality, market-integrity condition, tradeability, and action
  eligibility are separate dimensions. None may silently replace another.
- Conditions and transitions repeat; exact prices do not. Future comparison must
  be condition-based and categorical, not nominal-price imitation.
- Manipulation never automatically authorizes or rejects an action.
- Wallet and participant authenticity remains `UNKNOWN` when unproven.
- Missing governed evidence must remain `CURRENT_EVIDENCE_GAP` or
  `UNKNOWN_REQUIRES_RESEARCH`; Printer must not invent source capability.
- `WINDOW_5M_MICRO_EVENT` remains support-only and permanently
  non-authoritative. It cannot be a main outcome memory, replace 15m,
  independently trigger continuation, count toward main clean-memory thresholds,
  unlock retrieval, or unlock financial capability.
- All 19 V2-9.7C requirements from
  `docs/printer-v1-v2-9-7c-0-money-usefulness-requirements-readiness-audit.md`
  remain mandatory design inputs.
- All eight manipulation behaviours from the V2-9.7C.0 audit remain mandatory
  design coverage.
- All twelve tradeable-path contexts from the V2-9.7C.0 audit remain mandatory
  design coverage.

The eight manipulation behaviours that must remain covered by V2-9.7C design
are:

- fast coordinated pump
- wash-like or artificial flow
- concentrated early ownership or suspected insider distribution
- liquidity add, pull, lock, or unlock behavior
- dev, creator, pool, or holder pressure where governed evidence supports it
- trap, wick, late-buy, or exit-failure behavior
- dead-to-active, manipulated-to-active, or revival behavior
- participant-authenticity uncertainty

The twelve tradeable-path contexts that must remain covered by V2-9.7C design
are:

- expansion, pullback, continuation
- expansion then failed continuation
- fast breakdown then genuine reclaim
- wick-only peak
- price rising while exit deteriorates
- high volume with weak authenticity
- good entry with bad hold
- bad entry with profitable outcome
- correct exit then more upside
- missed entry with no-chase
- re-entry churn
- market-context mismatch

## Locks Preserved

These laws bind future design and later implementation. They do not unlock
runtime, source fetching, memory generation, retrieval, paper decisions,
BUY/SELL/HOLD, positions, trades, audits, PnL, live trading, wallets, private
keys, real funds, paid APIs, scoring, ranking, confidence percentages, weighted
logic, embeddings, vectors, operational commands, or 5m authority.
