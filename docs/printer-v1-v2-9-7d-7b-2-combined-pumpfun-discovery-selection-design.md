## Design Goal

One bounded campaign cycle may consider four factual intake channels:

- latest Pump.fun launches;
- provider-observed trending Pump.fun tokens;
- provider-observed top Pump.fun tokens;
- actively traded Pump.fun tokens.

The only allowed flow is:

`governed discovery -> identity merge -> fixed gates -> uniform selection -> tracking`

Every network operation belongs to Source Governor. Every unit of work belongs
to Central Scheduler. Selection has exactly two active slots as capacity and
uses no provider rank, trend position, boost, risk score, response order,
confidence, weighting, or hidden priority total.

## Frozen Provider Roles

| Provider | Frozen role | Authority | Operational state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct Pump.fun on-chain | Latest launches and primary Pump.fun-origin authority | A successful finalized Pump Program `create` transaction for the exact mint | Blocked until decoder/continuity contract adoption and implementation |
| DexScreener | Actively traded candidate discovery and exact-market enrichment | Provider market observation only | Existing governed source is reusable after combined-owner implementation |
| GeckoTerminal | Trending and active-pool candidate discovery | Provider pool observation only | Blocked until the adopted fixture-only contract is repaired |
| Solana Tracker free REST | Trending and top Pump.fun candidate discovery | Provider list observation only | Blocked until free-REST contract adoption and implementation |
| PumpSwap | Graduated-token pool confirmation | Exact PumpSwap program/pool relationship only | Confirmation-only; never candidate origin |
| PumpPortal | None while access requires incompatible wallet/funds state | None | Blocked; no request is planned |
| Pumpdev | None | None | Excluded from automatic execution |

Direct on-chain evidence is primary for Pump.fun origin, not automatically for
market tradeability, safety, selection, continuation, or action eligibility.
Third-party discovery can identify a mint for origin verification, but cannot
assert Pump.fun origin by label, URL, category, rank, or venue name alone.

## Frozen Channel Meanings

The four channel labels are provenance labels, not comparative quality:

- `LATEST_PUMPFUN`: exact finalized Pump Program creation observed between the
  persisted cursor boundary and cycle cutoff.
- `TRENDING_PUMPFUN`: mint appeared in an adopted GeckoTerminal trending-pool
  or Solana Tracker Pump.fun-trending response.
- `TOP_PUMPFUN`: mint appeared in an adopted Solana Tracker Pump.fun-top
  response.
- `ACTIVE_PUMPFUN`: an adopted DexScreener or GeckoTerminal exact-market
  response reports at least one transaction in its current bounded activity
  interval and meets freshness/provenance requirements.

A mint may carry multiple channel labels. Multiple labels do not increase its
selection probability. A provider's omission does not prove inactivity,
unpopularity, or rejection. "Trending" and "top" remain descriptions of the
provider response and never become Printer rankings.

## Immutable Cycle Inputs

Before intake, the execution owner must load and exact-match:

- campaign, configuration, run, and cycle identities;
- cycle ordinal and immutable cycle cutoff;
- policy version and exact provider-contract versions;
- explicit DB target and launch Git provenance;
- exactly two slot identities and their current occupancy;
- campaign selection seed;
- discovery cursor identity and prior finalized slot/signature boundary;
- per-kind source-operation, scheduler-work, duration, storage, and failure
  ceilings;
- Source Governor, Central Scheduler, lease, cancellation, and first-fault
  owners;
- persisted token, pair, lifecycle, and B.3 cooldown state.

Missing or mismatched input blocks the cycle before source work. The campaign
seed may not be generated by the executor or selector.

## Finite Intake Ceilings

These are maximums, never targets. They are an intake sub-budget inside the
larger immutable 7A campaign ceilings.

### Per-Cycle Source-Operation Ceiling

| Lane | Maximum governed calls | Per-operation timeout | Additional bound |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Direct Pump.fun live session | 1 | 10s connect, then 30s capture | One session; no reconnect/retry in the cycle |
| Direct finalized signature backfill | 2 | 10s | At most two pages |
| Direct create-transaction decoding | 16 | 10s | At most 16 launch signatures |
| Secondary-mint origin lookup | 8 | 10s | One lookup per verification-admitted mint |
| Secondary-mint origin transaction | 8 | 10s | One transaction per admitted mint |
| DexScreener fresh profiles plus batch | 2 | 8s | One profile request and one batch request |
| GeckoTerminal trending plus active enrichment | 2 | 10s | Zero until contract repair |
| Solana Tracker trending plus top | 2 | 10s | Zero until contract adoption |
| PumpSwap confirmation | 4 | 10s | At most four migration claims |
| **Total** | **45** | | Hard per-cycle intake ceiling |

Underlying JSON-RPC operations are counted separately inside the direct lane.
A governed request that fans out may not conceal operation usage. The direct
lane also has an underlying RPC-operation ceiling of 45 for the cycle. If an
adopted adapter needs more than one RPC operation for an item, it must reduce
the admitted item count or propose a later explicit ceiling change; it may not
silently exceed 45.

### Other Per-Cycle Ceilings

- Scheduler intake work: 11 rows.
- Normalized provider observations: 96.
- Unique merged mint identities: 64.
- Secondary-only mints admitted to origin verification: 8.
- PumpSwap migration claims admitted to confirmation: 4.
- Selected tracking handoffs: 2.
- Canonical raw payload: 1 MiB per response.
- Total persisted intake growth: 8 MiB per cycle.
- Provider-lane failures: 5; a sixth failure request is not created.
- Intake wall-clock deadline: 360 seconds from cycle `DISCOVERING` entry.
- Ordinary source retries: 0.
- Ordinary scheduler retries: 0.

The immutable campaign configuration must reserve:

`intake_source_calls = cycle_count * 45`

`intake_scheduler_work = cycle_count * 11`

`intake_storage_bytes = cycle_count * 8 MiB`

These reservations are separate from approved tracking/window source and work
budgets. A 7A global ceiling smaller than the sum of all frozen sub-budgets
blocks preflight. Unused intake budget cannot be converted into extra tracking,
source retries, larger provider pages, or additional tokens.

## Direct Pump.fun Decoder and Continuity

### Decoder Contract

The implementation must pin:

- exact official Pump repository commit and IDL SHA-256;
- Pump Program ID;
- `create` instruction discriminator and account order;
- emitted creation-event discriminator and field layout where used;
- supported transaction versions;
- exact mint, bonding-curve, associated bonding-curve, creator-address,
  signature, slot, block-time, and program identities.

Only a successful transaction at explicit `finalized` commitment can establish
`PUMPFUN_ORIGIN_CONFIRMED`. The requested mint must equal the decoded create
mint. Failed transactions, wrong programs, malformed accounts, null
transactions, unsupported versions, missing finality, or ambiguous create
instructions produce a visible gap and no origin claim.

Creator is an observed address only. The decoder cannot infer wallet control,
coordination, insider status, authenticity, intent, beneficial ownership, or
participant identity.

### Cursor and Cutoff

The cursor is immutable history with:

- cluster and RPC endpoint identity;
- Pump Program and decoder-contract identity;
- last contiguous finalized slot;
- last processed signature within that slot;
- cycle cutoff slot/time;
- backfill page count and first/last covered signatures;
- continuity state: `CONTIGUOUS`, `GAPPED`, or `UNKNOWN`;
- exact request/response/failure and scheduler-work links.

The cycle cutoff is resolved once. Post-cutoff signatures belong to a later
cycle and cannot rewrite the current intake.

### Stream Interruption, Reconnect, and Backfill

Operational design permits zero ordinary retries. Therefore:

1. The cycle opens one bounded live subscription work item.
2. If it disconnects, the work item fails and is not reconnected in that cycle.
3. The already-planned finalized signature backfill starts from the persisted
   cursor and advances only through the immutable cycle cutoff.
4. If two pages cannot establish contiguous coverage, the cursor remains
   `GAPPED`; unsupported launches remain unknown and no completeness claim is
   made.
5. The next scheduled cycle may open a new subscription after first backfilling
   from the unchanged contiguous cursor. That is new planned cycle work, not a
   retry, resume, or background reconnect loop.

Live observations at lower commitment are provisional only. They are not
merged as authoritative launches until the corresponding transaction is
successfully verified at `finalized`. Duplicate signatures are idempotent.
Forked, missing, or failed provisional observations remain explicit failures.

## Provider Acquisition Rules

### Direct Latest

The direct lane accepts at most 16 finalized create transactions in the cycle
boundary. It preserves additional bounded signatures as
`NOT_DECODED_SOURCE_CEILING` gaps. No provider ordering decides which signatures
are decoded: signatures are ordered by `(slot, transaction_index, signature)`
in ascending event time before the 16-item ceiling is applied.

### DexScreener Active

The lane obtains one bounded fresh-profile response and one exact-mint batch
enrichment. It preserves profile/listing time separately from pair creation and
provider observation time. Boosts, profile order, labels, and provider
visibility are removed from the normalized eligibility object.

DexScreener cannot prove mint creation, Pump.fun origin, migration, participant
identity, or token age. Active-channel attribution requires a fresh exact-pair
response and at least one reported transaction in the provider's current
bounded activity interval.

### GeckoTerminal Trending and Active

This lane is planned but forced to `SKIPPED_BLOCKED_CONTRACT` until the adopted
fixture-only blocker is repaired. After repair it may contribute exact pool,
trending-channel, and bounded activity observations. Provider trend order,
engagement, security/credibility inputs, and any derived ordering are excluded
from the normalized eligibility object.

### Solana Tracker Trending and Top

This lane is planned but forced to `SKIPPED_BLOCKED_CONTRACT` until free REST,
authentication, exact Pump.fun filtering, schema, quota, and provenance
contracts pass adoption. Paid Data Stream/WebSocket access is forbidden.

Provider rank, score, risk, popularity, order, and promoted fields are retained
only in raw immutable provenance. They are omitted from merged candidates,
gate inputs, seed derivation, canonical ordering, and reports other than an
explicit `discarded_non_authoritative_fields` list.

### PumpSwap Confirmation

PumpSwap is queried only for an origin-confirmed mint that claims a graduated
Pump lifecycle and has exact migration-signature evidence. Confirmation must
resolve one unique PumpSwap pool owned by the exact PumpSwap program with the
exact base mint. Failure or ambiguity blocks that market identity only.

PumpSwap cannot discover the mint, establish creation time, supply trend/top
status, or choose a candidate.

## Canonical Identities

### Root Identity

Every intake fact exact-links:

`campaign_id / configuration_id / run_id / cycle_id / discovery_batch_id`

and carries policy version, cycle cutoff, Git provenance identity, scheduler
work identity, Source Governor request/response/failure identity, source name,
request kind, capture time, and canonical payload hash.

### Token Identity

`token_identity = solana-mainnet:<canonical-base58-mint>`

Symbol, name, provider slug, URL, creator label, and display metadata never
participate in equality.

### Market Identity

`market_identity = solana-mainnet:<venue_program>:<pair_or_pool_address>`

It also carries exact base mint, quote mint, base/quote orientation, venue, and
lifecycle phase. A token is not tracking-eligible without one exact market
identity.

A Pump bonding-curve account is not silently written as an AMM pair. A latest
launch without an adopted exact market/pair representation remains
`DISCOVERED_UNPAIRED` and ineligible. A later implementation lane must either
prove the existing pair contract can represent that venue explicitly or add
the smallest distinct market link. It may not overload pair semantics.

### Lifecycle Identity

Fixed intake lifecycle states are:

- `PUMP_CREATED_UNPAIRED`
- `PUMP_BONDING_CURVE_ACTIVE`
- `PUMP_MIGRATION_OBSERVED`
- `PUMPSWAP_GRADUATED_CONFIRMED`
- `PUMP_LIFECYCLE_UNKNOWN`

These intake states describe venue progression only. They do not replace B.3
tracking lifecycle authority.

## Identity Merge

Merge is deterministic and side-effect free before persistence:

1. Validate canonical source payload and provenance.
2. Normalize cluster, mint, venue, market address, base/quote sides, observed
   time, and provider channel labels.
3. Strip non-authoritative rank/order/score/boost/risk fields from gate input.
4. Group by exact token identity.
5. Within a token, group by exact market identity and lifecycle phase.
6. Sort provider contributions by
   `(source_name, request_kind, observed_at, response_id, payload_hash)`.
7. Build one immutable merged candidate plus explicit conflicts/gaps.

Provider multiplicity never duplicates a mint in the selection pool and never
increases probability.

### Exact Duplicate

The same mint, market, lifecycle phase, and immutable observation identity is
one duplicate set. All provenance remains linked; factual fields are not
counted twice.

### Migration

A same-mint/new-pair transition is `MIGRATION_CONFIRMED` only when:

- Pump.fun origin is confirmed;
- an exact successful migration transaction links the predecessor market;
- the successor pool is uniquely confirmed by PumpSwap;
- event time is after predecessor creation and no later than cycle cutoff.

Both predecessor and successor remain stored. The successor does not rewrite
the earlier market.

### Same Mint / New Pair

Without exact migration evidence, a new pair is one of:

- `DISTINCT_MARKET_SUPPORTED` only under a separately adopted venue contract;
- `REVIVAL_SUPPORTED` only after B.3 cooldown/archive reconciliation and fresh
  governed evidence;
- `PAIR_DRIFT_CONFLICT`;
- `SAME_MINT_NEW_PAIR_UNKNOWN`.

Only the first two may proceed, and neither is inferred by provider consensus.
For this first combined intake, unadopted distinct-market and revival claims
remain ineligible.

### Conflicts

Conflicting cluster, mint, base/quote orientation, Pump origin, creation
transaction, migration link, or market owner is never majority-voted. The
candidate becomes `IDENTITY_CONFLICT` and fails gates. A cross-campaign/run/
cycle provenance link, canonical payload mutation, or database ownership
conflict is a shared first fault and stops campaign work safely.

## Verification Admission Before Expensive Gates

Secondary feeds may return more mints than the origin-verification budget. This
resource step cannot use provider rank or response order.

1. Merge exact secondary observations by token identity.
2. Exclude mints already carrying direct finalized origin evidence.
3. Canonical-sort by token identity.
4. Compute
   `verification_key = SHA256(cycle_selection_seed || "|origin|" || token_identity)`.
5. Sort by `(verification_key, token_identity)`.
6. Admit the first eight mints for origin verification.

Remaining mints are preserved as
`ORIGIN_VERIFICATION_NOT_ADMITTED_CEILING`; they are not rejected as unsafe and
cannot enter final selection. Provider duplication does not add entries or
improve admission probability.

PumpSwap claims use the same process with domain separator `"|pumpswap|"` and
a four-mint ceiling.

## Fixed Eligibility Gates

Gates execute in this exact order and stop at the first failed gate while
retaining all reasons:

1. **Ownership:** exact campaign/configuration/run/cycle/discovery-batch and
   cutoff linkage.
2. **Source provenance:** approved source/request kind, request before
   transport, immutable response/failure, canonical hash, and Scheduler owner.
3. **Solana identity:** mainnet, canonical mint, valid exact market address,
   exact base/quote orientation, and supported venue.
4. **Pump.fun origin:** `PUMPFUN_ORIGIN_CONFIRMED` from finalized on-chain
   evidence for the exact mint.
5. **Lifecycle/market:** supported intake lifecycle and one exact current
   market; claimed graduation requires PumpSwap confirmation.
6. **Freshness/cutoff:** provider observation meets its adopted freshness
   contract and no required fact is post-cutoff.
7. **Evidence quality:** required facts are `COMPLETE/CLEAN_DATA` or an
   explicitly adopted `PARTIAL/ACCEPTABLE_PARTIAL_DATA` combination; mandatory
   gaps, stale data, and dirty data fail.
8. **Candidate role:** at least one supported latest, trending, top, or active
   channel label remains after normalization.
9. **Infrastructure exclusion:** infrastructure tokens, invalid assets,
   unsupported quote orientation, WATCH_ONLY, and audit-only candidates cannot
   enter active selection.
10. **Duplicate/conflict:** one token and current market identity, no unresolved
    same-mint/new-pair condition, and no identity conflict.
11. **B.3 reconciliation:** a replacement candidate cannot occupy a slot until
    terminal reconciliation leaves zero active associated work.
12. **Cooldown:** token and pair each satisfy the committed three-selection-
    batch cooldown; same-pair recycling remains blocked.
13. **Vacancy:** the target slot is vacant and the total active capacity remains
    exactly two.
14. **Budget:** final selection and handoff fit all remaining immutable source,
    scheduler, duration, storage, and failure ceilings.

Safety, manipulation, memory quality, continuation, and action eligibility are
not discovery gates unless an existing higher-authority intake contract
explicitly requires a factual safety exclusion. Manipulation context cannot
select or reject a token. No gate calculates a score.

## Immutable Seed and Uniform Selection

The configuration stores a non-empty `campaign_selection_seed`. For each batch:

`cycle_selection_seed = SHA256(`
`"PrinterV1|combined-pumpfun-v1|" + campaign_selection_seed + "|" +`
`campaign_id + "|" + run_id + "|" + cycle_id + "|" + discovery_batch_id`
`)`

The executor rejects a missing seed. It never calls the current selector with
`seed=None`.

After every fixed gate:

1. Collapse to one eligible current market per mint using only exact lifecycle
   authority; ambiguity blocks the mint.
2. Canonical-sort by `(token_identity, market_identity, lifecycle_identity)`.
3. Apply a deterministic Fisher-Yates shuffle using the exact
   `cycle_selection_seed`.
4. Select without replacement for the exact vacancy count.

Initial activation has two vacancies and requires two eligible candidates. If
fewer than two exist, it creates no partial active campaign and records
`INSUFFICIENT_ELIGIBLE_TWO_SLOT_POOL`. During rotation, the healthy occupied
slot remains untouched and selection targets only the reconciled vacant slot.
Zero vacancies produces no selection or tracking handoff.

Old bucket/quota output may remain diagnostic for historical reports, but it
is not a gate and cannot alter the shuffled result. Provider contributions,
channel count, trend/top labels, and raw response order cannot reserve slots.

## Transactional Tracking Handoff

For each selected vacancy, one transaction must:

- compare the campaign, run, cycle, slot, and selection-batch states;
- verify the slot remains vacant and the other slot identity is unchanged;
- persist exact token, mint, market/pair, venue, lifecycle, and selection links;
- persist the selection seed and canonical selected-candidate hash;
- create the existing tracking-queue handoff;
- create the Central Scheduler first-15m job;
- transition the slot to tracking.

The transaction rolls back on any mismatch or duplicate active work. Repeated
identical handoff is idempotent. Conflicting handoff fails closed. One
token-local handoff failure does not mutate the healthy slot; database,
ownership, lease, or ceiling failure invokes campaign safe stop.

Every selected token starts one main `WINDOW_15M`. No discovery channel can
trigger 1h/4h continuation or 5m support capture.

## Failure Isolation

| Failure | Required result |
|---|---|
| Direct live disconnect | Fail live work, run planned bounded finalized backfill, no same-cycle reconnect |
| Direct backfill remains gapped | Preserve gap; do not claim complete latest coverage |
| Direct origin authority unavailable | Secondary observations persist, but no unverified mint passes origin gate; no replacement selection |
| DexScreener failure | Active/profile contribution unavailable; other adopted lanes continue |
| GeckoTerminal failure | Trending/active contribution unavailable; other lanes continue |
| Solana Tracker failure | Trending/top contribution unavailable; other lanes continue |
| PumpSwap failure/ambiguity | Claimed graduated candidate blocked; non-migrated candidates unaffected |
| Provider payload too large | Provider lane fails before persistence beyond its payload ceiling |
| Source/scheduler/failure ceiling reached | Create no further work; preserve first cause and begin safe cleanup as applicable |
| Candidate identity conflict | Candidate-local block unless it exposes cross-owner/cross-campaign corruption |
| Cross-campaign/run/cycle or immutable payload mismatch | Shared first fault; cooperative cancellation and campaign safe stop |
| Source Governor or Central Scheduler unavailable | Preflight/shared stop; no adapter fallback |
| Lease renewal unconfirmed | Stop new and active child work through committed supervision |

Provider failure never authorizes another provider to fill authoritative fields.
No endpoint rotation occurs after identity, parser, data-quality, or provenance
failure. There are no ordinary retries.

## Provider-Contribution Report

The cycle and final campaign report must include, per provider/request kind:

- planned state and contract version;
- skipped/blocked reason;
- request, response, failure, and scheduler-work identities;
- attempted/completed/failed/cancelled operation counts;
- underlying RPC-operation count where applicable;
- bytes stored and source-duration usage;
- observations received and normalized;
- raw duplicate count and merged unique-mint contribution;
- candidates admitted/not admitted to origin or PumpSwap verification;
- candidates passing/failing each fixed gate;
- channel labels contributed;
- authoritative facts contributed;
- selected slot links;
- discarded non-authoritative field names;
- continuity cursor, backfill coverage, and visible gaps.

Counts are factual diagnostics only. The report must not compare provider
quality, calculate yield, rank providers, or use contribution counts in
selection. Unknown, unavailable, and skipped providers remain visible.

## 7A Execution-Owner Binding

7A remains the command/preflight/supervision/report owner. It must continue to
receive Source Governor and Central Scheduler ports and call exactly one
injected campaign executor.

The later combined execution owner implements:

`CombinedPumpfunCampaignExecutor.execute(command, source_governor, central_scheduler)`

It must:

1. load the exact runnable campaign graph and immutable intake configuration;
2. validate per-cycle sub-budgets against 7A global ceilings;
3. create only Central Scheduler-owned cycle work;
4. send every transport through Source Governor;
5. run deterministic merge, gates, and selection as pure evaluations;
6. perform transactional slot handoff;
7. return exact usage, owner-use, selective-continuation, support-only 5m,
   terminal, successor, and restart evidence expected by 7A.

7A must not import provider adapters, normalize payloads, merge identities,
select candidates, or create tracking jobs directly. Report-only mode continues
to call zero-source read-only replay and never instantiates this owner.

## Required Persistence Links

Current migration 032 campaign scheduler work is token/window rooted and allows
one source request link. Discovery occurs before a token slot/window assignment
and one provider work item may own multiple governed requests. It must not be
forced into fabricated token/window identities.

The minimum later persistence reconciliation must provide:

- one campaign/run/cycle-rooted discovery batch with cutoff, cursor, policy,
  contract versions, campaign seed identity, derived cycle seed, state, and
  canonical hash;
- cycle-rooted discovery work exact-linked to an existing Scheduler job and
  immutable work type/deadline/state;
- a one-to-many immutable work-to-source-fact link for request, response, and
  failure rows;
- immutable normalized provider observations with token/market/lifecycle
  identities, observed time, raw payload hash, and provenance;
- one merged-candidate object with contribution links, conflicts, gaps, and
  canonical hash;
- exact origin-verification and PumpSwap-confirmation links;
- campaign/run/cycle/discovery-batch links from the existing selection batch;
- selected-item to exact token-slot and tracking-handoff links;
- provider-contribution report-object links for final report and replay.

Existing source rows, Scheduler jobs, migration 025 selection ownership,
campaign root, B.3 lifecycle, B.4 supervision, B.5 Git provenance, and 4A-5C
object owners remain authoritative. The later schema lane must prefer junction
links over copied semantic fields and prove whether a distinct pre-graduation
market identity is necessary.

## State Flow

```text
cycle PLANNED
  -> DISCOVERING
     -> provider work (independent)
     -> direct finalized continuity reconciliation
  -> MERGING
     -> normalized observations
     -> exact mint/market/lifecycle merge
  -> VERIFYING
     -> deterministic origin-verification admission
     -> Pump.fun origin verification
     -> bounded PumpSwap confirmation
  -> SELECTING
     -> ordered fixed gates
     -> token/pair cooldown
     -> canonical uniform selection for vacancies
  -> TRACKING
     -> transactional slot handoff
     -> first WINDOW_15M scheduler work
```

If initial two-slot selection cannot be filled, the cycle becomes
`TERMINAL_BLOCKED` without partial activation. A replacement cycle may leave
one healthy token tracking while one vacancy remains unfilled.

## Implementation Lanes

Implementation must proceed in this order:

1. **7B.3A direct Pump contract adoption:** pin program/IDL, transaction/event
   decoding, finalized evidence, cursor, gap, and operation accounting.
2. **7B.3B secondary contract adoption:** repair GeckoTerminal and adopt Solana
   Tracker free REST. Recheck PumpPortal; keep it blocked if wallet/funds are
   still required. Pumpdev remains excluded.
3. **7B.4A direct adapter/continuity implementation:** fixture-only decoder,
   cursor, bounded backfill, zero-retry failure behavior, and governed
   operation accounting.
4. **7B.4B secondary adapters:** implement only adopted GeckoTerminal and
   Solana Tracker lanes; reuse DexScreener and PumpSwap owners without
   expanding their authority.
5. **7B.4C persistence reconciliation:** add only the proven cycle-rooted
   discovery/work/source/merge/selection/report links.
6. **7B.4D combined execution owner:** bind provider work, pure merge/gates,
   immutable seed selection, transactional handoff, and 7A result evidence.
7. **7B.5 isolated combined proof:** use disposable databases and fixtures to
   prove all contracts together.
8. **7B.6 bounded live-source proof:** only after explicit operator approval,
   provider by provider before a combined low-ceiling proof.
9. **Activation review:** separately decide persistent migration, command
   publication, and pilot readiness. No prior lane grants activation.

Each implementation lane requires its own focused tests and PASS-only closeout.

## Proof Gates

### Contract Gate

- Exact pinned provider schema/auth/quota/version evidence.
- No paid dependency, wallet, funds, signing, or execution requirement.
- Every request kind and evidence field has explicit authority.
- Rank/order/score/boost/risk stripping is fixture-proven.

### Direct Decoder Gate

- Valid create, wrong program, failed transaction, malformed accounts,
  unsupported version, duplicate signature, post-cutoff event, missing
  finality, fork, null transaction, and exact-mint mismatch.
- Contiguous cursor, stream disconnect, two-page backfill, bounded gap,
  next-cycle subscription, and deterministic replay.
- Governed request and underlying RPC-operation ceilings both hold.

### Merge and Selection Gate

- All four channels represented without authority confusion.
- Duplicate provider observations do not multiply selection probability.
- Migration, same-mint/new-pair, distinct market, revival, and conflict cases.
- Deterministic verification admission independent of provider order.
- Every fixed gate and committed three-batch token/pair cooldown.
- Missing seed rejected; identical input produces identical bytes and selection.
- Provider rank/order/score/boost/risk changes do not change selection.
- Initial cycle selects exactly two or none; replacement fills only vacancies.

### Ownership and Failure Gate

- Every network operation has Source Governor and Scheduler provenance.
- Independent secondary failures do not erase healthy observations.
- Direct authority loss blocks unsupported candidates without inventing origin.
- Shared identity, DB, owner, lease, or ceiling failure preserves first fault
  and completes committed cleanup.
- No retry, endpoint rotation, resume, successor, or automatic restart.

### Persistence, Report, and Replay Gate

- Exact campaign/run/cycle/discovery/selection/slot/handoff foreign links.
- Canonical payload immutability and conflicting-repeat rejection.
- Provider contribution, discarded-field, continuity, gap, and budget reports.
- Deterministic final campaign report.
- Zero-source replay preserves all unknowns/gaps and performs zero calls, work,
  memory writes, and database writes.
- Locked-capability baseline/final deltas are exactly zero.

### Live-Proof Gate

- Separate low-ceiling source probes after operator approval.
- Real schema, authentication, free quota, timeout, and rate-limit behavior.
- Direct finalized decoding and bounded continuity over an interruption.
- Provider failure isolation before any combined proof.
- No persistent target, operational campaign, or pilot until a separate
  activation review passes.

## Money-Usefulness Contribution

This design broadens the factual candidate universe while keeping Pump.fun
origin anchored to finalized on-chain evidence and market activity anchored to
exact provider observations. Uniform selection after fixed gates reduces
provider-promotion and response-order bias, while cooldown and exact
migration identity improve corpus diversity without manufacturing relevance.

The contribution is cleaner intake for later memory collection. It does not
predict profit, authorize an action, or claim that trending/top tokens are
better candidates.

## Functionality Risks / Setbacks / Efficiency Blockers

- The 45-call ceiling is a conservative design budget, not measured live
  capacity. Contract and fixture proof may require reducing candidate counts.
- Public RPC counts top-level governed requests differently from underlying
  JSON-RPC operations. Both ceilings must be implemented before live proof.
- A two-page backfill can leave legitimate launch coverage gapped; the design
  preserves the gap instead of expanding unboundedly.
- Secondary-only origin verification admits eight mints uniformly per cycle.
  Other valid candidates remain unverified due to budget and cannot be selected.
- Provider new/trending/top feeds remain curated subsets even after rank and
  order are stripped, so observed intake is not a complete Pump.fun population.
- Exact pair semantics for pre-graduation bonding curves are not proven in the
  current pair schema. Unpaired launches remain ineligible until resolved.
- Same-mint/new-pair evidence may represent migration, revival, distinct
  markets, drift, or manipulation. Unsupported cases remain blocked.
- Current migration 032 work ownership is token/window rooted and cannot
  truthfully own pre-selection work without a narrow persistence repair.
- The persistent target remains behind the committed campaign schema and
  cannot run this design.
- Free provider authentication, quotas, and schemas can drift. A later
  activation must revalidate the exact adopted versions.
